## Link-building actions (realistic, local Romanian dental clinic)
These are general recommendations, not derived from observed gaps.

Priority High
1. Request link updates from every site that links to drpetric.ro: local directories, press, professional bodies, and the GBP and social profiles. A direct link carries more than a redirect.
2. Make NAP (name, address, phone) consistent across the Romanian directories: Google Business Profile, Facebook, Pagini Aurii, Doctori.ro, Medicover-style health listings, Regina Maria-type marketplaces where dentists list, and local Satu Mare business directories. Remove the old drpetric.ro name and URL.
3. Local press: pitch Satu Mare outlets (e.g. local news sites and radio) with real stories, such as a free dental check day, oral health advice for schools, or new equipment. Offer a named dentist as an expert source.

Priority Medium
4. Professional associations: the Colegiul Medicilor Dentisti (CMDR) Satu Mare branch, and any implantology or aesthetic dentistry associations the team belongs to, often have member pages with links.
5. Local partners: orthodontists, labs, pharmacies, gyms, beauty salons, wedding and events businesses, and employers offering staff discounts. Ask for a "partener" page link in exchange for a reciprocal listing.
6. Sponsorships: local sports clubs, school events, charity runs, and community festivals. Sponsor pages usually include a link.
7. Guest content: oral health articles for local community or parenting sites, with the dentist's byline and bio.

Priority Low
8. University and school collaborations: talks or an internship programme, which can earn a link from a faculty or school page.
9. Reviews and testimonials: give a quote for the dental suppliers and equipment vendors you use. They often link to the clinic.
10. Avoid paid link packages and private blog networks. The profile is small, so a spammy link would hurt more than it helps.
